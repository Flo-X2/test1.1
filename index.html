How it works

From scoping to action plan

Week 1

Scope

We meet the chair and company secretary, agree the evaluation types and tailor the questionnaire to your sector and constitution.

Weeks 2–4

Survey

Directors and selected executives complete a confidential online questionnaire in about 30 minutes. We send reminders and track completion.

Weeks 4–5

Interviews

Optional one‑to‑one conversations with each director to test the survey findings and surface what scores can't show.

Weeks 6–8

Report & debrief

A written report, a facilitated board session, and an action plan with owners and dates that we check in on after 6 and 12 months.

The framework

Six dimensions, scored 1 to 5

Questions are drawn from the NZX Corporate Governance Code, IoD guidance and the Four Pillars of governance best practice, then adjusted for your organisation.

D1
Strategic direction

The board as a compass: setting the ethical direction and keeping the entity on course.

D2
Composition & skills

Is the right mix of skills, experience and diversity around the table?

D3
Chair leadership

Does the chair run effective meetings and draw out every voice?

D4
Culture & dynamics

Is there trust, constructive challenge and room for disagreement?

D5
Information & meetings

Are papers timely and decision‑ready, and is the agenda focused on the future?

D6
Oversight of management

Is the board‑CEO relationship clear, with healthy accountability?

Common questions

What boards usually ask us

Who sees the results?

The full board receives the report. Individual director feedback goes only to that director and the chair. Raw survey answers and interview notes stay with us.

Are the responses really anonymous?

Yes. We only report results for groups of three or more and paraphrase any comment that could identify someone. On very small boards we talk this through with you at the start.

How much does a review cost?

Each review has a fixed fee, agreed before we start. It depends on the size of the board and which evaluations you choose. A short conversation is enough for us to quote.

How long does it take?

Most facilitated reviews take six to eight weeks from the first conversation to the board debrief. A self-assessment can be done in three to four weeks.

How often should a board be reviewed?

Many boards do a short self-assessment every year and an independent external review every two to three years.

What if a review uncovers a serious issue?

A board review looks at how the board works, not at misconduct or legal questions. If something serious comes up, we raise it with the chair and recommend the right specialist adviser.

Do you work with iwi and Māori entities?

Yes. We shape the approach with you and involve people with the right cultural expertise, rather than applying a standard corporate framework.

The story behind the name

The same wind, a different welcome

In ancient Greece, Notus was the south wind, and few were glad to see him. He blew in hot and dusty off the desert at the end of summer, brought the autumn storms and was feared as a destroyer of crops.

Down here the south wind comes from the other direction. It crosses thousands of kilometres of open Southern Ocean before it reaches Aotearoa, and it arrives cool, clean and clearing. From Northland to Southland, a southerly change breaks the heat, washes the haze from the sky and leaves the air crisp and clear: the kind of day when you can see for miles.

North of the equator

A wind to fear

  • Hot and dusty, off the desert
  • Brings late‑summer storms
  • Blamed for ruined harvests
South of the equator

A wind to welcome

  • Cool and pure, off the Southern Ocean
  • Breaks the heat and the dry spell
  • Brings rain, relief and fresh air

The wind is the same. What changes is where you stand. That is how we start every review: from where your board stands.

What the southerly brings to a board

Clarity

Clear air

A southerly change cuts through sticky heat in minutes. A good review does the same for a board, clearing away assumptions and leaving an honest picture of what's working and what isn't.

Independence

Nothing picked up on the way

The southerly crosses open ocean with nothing to taint it. We arrive the same way: we don't do audit, legal or recruitment work for the boards we review.

Direction

Steering by the south

Southern navigators find south by the Southern Cross. Our framework gives your board a fixed point to steer by and measure against, year after year.

Clear air for better boards. Clear thinking, independent advice and a fixed point to steer by.

Privacy

Privacy policy

Draft for review. This explains how Notus Governance handles personal information under the Privacy Act 2020.

What we collect

When you contact us, we collect your name, email address and anything you include in your message. During a review, we collect directors' names, roles and contact details, their survey answers and our interview notes.

How we use it

We use contact details to reply to you and to run the engagement you have asked for. Survey answers and interview notes are used only to prepare that board's review. We never sell personal information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Confidentiality

Survey results are reported only for groups of three or more, and comments are paraphrased if they could identify someone. Raw answers and interview notes are never shared with the board.

Storage and retention

We store information on secure systems hosted in New Zealand or Australia. We keep raw survey data and interview notes for 12 months after the final report, so the next review can be compared, and then securely destroy them.

Your rights

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you by emailing office@notusgovernance.co.nz. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.
